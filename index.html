Our Story

हमारी कहानी — नेपाल से भारत तक का सफर

Origin of RumPum

The group started with Asian Thai Foods (P) Ltd. in 1999 in Biratnagar, Nepal. The corporate office is located in Kathmandu and the first factory was set up in Biratnagar, Nepal.

Asian Thai Foods is a joint venture between two Nepal Based Groups — the Sarda Group and the Jaju Group. Products are consumed in more than 150 cities and townships in Nepal, major cities of Northern and North Eastern states of India, and over 10 cities in Bhutan.

Saraogi Group based in Jhansi (Uttar Pradesh) since 1980s, led by Mr. Pawan Kumar Saraogi, has had varied successful business experience. Annual turnover ranging between INR 100-125 crores.
